###Break and Continue Statements in JavaScript

##About the Task

This task demonstrates the use of break and continue statements in JavaScript.

The programs use loops, conditional statements, digit operations, ranges, and nested loops to understand how `break` and `continue` control the execution of a loop.

##Concepts Covered

* `break` statement
* `continue` statement
* `for` loop
* `while` loop
* Nested loops
* Conditional statements
* Range-based programs
* Digit extraction
* Prime numbers
* Palindrome numbers
* Perfect numbers
* Divisibility
* Digit sum

##Break Programs

The break statement is used to immediately stop a loop when the required condition is satisfied.

Programs include:

* First even digit from the left
* First prime number between 50 and 100
* First number whose digit sum is 10
* First number with exactly 3 divisors
* First 3 consecutive odd numbers
* First palindrome between 10 and 500
* First perfect number from 1 to 1000
* First 5 even numbers
* First 5 prime numbers
* First 3 numbers divisible by 7

##Continue Programs

The continue statement is used to skip the current iteration and move to the next iteration of the loop.

Programs include:

* Print 1–30 by skipping even numbers
* Print 1–40 by skipping multiples of 4
* Print 1–30 by skipping numbers from 10–20
* Print 1–50 by skipping multiples of 3
* Extract a number by skipping 0
* Extract even digits
* Skip odd digits
* Skip multiples of 3 or 5
* Skip numbers with odd digit sum
* Skip numbers containing digit 0

##Break and Continue Together

The task also contains programs where both statements are used together.

Examples include:

* Skip multiples of 3 and stop at 40
* Skip even numbers and stop at the first multiple of 7
* Skip odd digits and stop when 0 is found
* Extract digits until 5 is found
* Search for the first multiple of 9

##Learning Outcome

Through this task, I learned how to control the flow of loops using `break` and `continue` and how to apply them in range programs, nested loops, and number-based problems.

Technology Used: JavaScript
